# Source scope and project assets

Original songs use original words, melodies and project-local generated audio.
Production references supply measurements and principles. Their recordings, stems,
transcribed notes/lyrics, covers and reconstructed scores stay private and separate
from distributed original demos, package fixtures and reusable sample catalogs.

For a requested recreation, use supplied material or the user's confirmed ownership
or permission within its stated scope. Record and reuse that scope for the task;
do not import authorization from another study or ask for the same confirmation
repeatedly. Neither possession of a recording nor this workflow grants new rights.

Preserve the source and accepted variants. Label sampled original audio, stock-voice
replacement and A/B exports accurately. An A/B containing source audio remains
source-derived even when the replacement is synthesized. Keep credentials, private
source locations and study permission records out of distributable resources.

Use project-relative outputs and returned artifact paths. Exporting a user's song
does not register it as a DAWG demo or publish it. Stock synthesis is distinct from
an artist's voice identity; do not present it as an identical singer or master.
