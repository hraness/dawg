# House-hyperpop profile

The authoritative numeric policy is [house-v1.json](../house-v1.json), exported by
the runtime as `HOUSE_PROFILE`. Workflow, chart and agent consumers share that
profile rather than maintaining separate tempo, harmony and register rules.

The house is energetic, sincere and varied: bubblegum, euphoric, driving or bright
bittersweet pop. Use the full profile tempo range rather than forcing every song
into a fast template. Keep one diatonic major scale with no borrowed chords or
modulation. Speech-shaped low verses lift into a sung chorus; avoid a permanently
high synthetic topline. British stock `bf_emma` is the lead, not an artist clone.

Write concrete, plain original words and a memorable hook. Start some lines on
pickups, hold stressed vowels, leave rests, and vary phrase lengths. Let drums
drive the song except a contrasting break. Develop each repeat with a new texture,
filter arc, fill, counterline or dropout. Bright plucked stabs should leave dry rests;
choruses may become sustained and wide. Keep low-mid masking and excessive air in check.

Original lyric rewrites may improve clarity. A cover retains its requested words.
The profile's intelligibility gates are independent of taste scores and technical
file checks. Future vocal-mix qualification may revise a versioned policy; this
resource does not change existing demo audio or prescribe a gain repair.
