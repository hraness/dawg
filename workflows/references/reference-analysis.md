# Timing and production evidence

Ingest once, then separate, analyze and transcribe bounded useful ranges. Consume
operation outputs instead of constructing stem paths. Cache only for exact source,
tool and configuration identities. A source locator and content hash are different.

Fit tempo/phase from actual onsets and verify downbeats against section entries and
repeat lengths. Map all source seconds through the saved grid origin. Transcription
tempos and quantized note fields may disagree with the full mix. Fold repeated parts
to reject sporadic detections; inspect pitch bends, octave flicker and drum bleed.

Measure form, active stems, drum patterns, register, pitch activity, loudness, frequency
bands, sustain, stereo width, filter arcs and sidechain timing. Record uncertainty
and confidence. A credible arrangement also needs timbre and dynamics, not just notes.

For original-music research, distill measurements into production principles without
copying riffs, melodies, lyric lines or bar-by-bar chord charts. For a requested private
recreation, keep the detailed reconstruction local and distinguish automatic draft,
refined score, source sample and synthesized replacement.
