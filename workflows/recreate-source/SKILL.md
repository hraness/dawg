# Recreate a source

Read [source use](../references/source-use.md), [reference analysis](../references/reference-analysis.md),
[vocal guidance](../references/vocals.md) and [readiness](../references/readiness.md).

1. Ingest the selected URL or local recording into the user's project. Preserve the
   source, its content hash, separated original vocal and scope of supplied or
   confirmed permission. Reuse that scope for this task; do not transfer another
   study's authorization. Keep the study private.
2. Separate and analyze; fit grid, origin and downbeats from audio, map sections
   and repeats, and retain uncertainty. Start an editable draft from transcription,
   then refine bass stability, drum classification, repeated parts and harmony.
   A quantized automatic draft is not a finished reconstruction.
3. Rebuild instrumental parts from notes. Default vocals to the actual lyrics
   re-sung by stock British `bf_emma`, retaining the original for explicit comparison.
   Cross-check words and timing against the source and supplied/authorized lyric
   material. Preserve nonlexical chops as synthesized nonlexical audio. Do not replace
   requested lyrics with new words, vocables or the sampled singer.
4. Derive source pitch, dynamics and phonetic timing. Use the stock voice's spectral
   envelope, not an artist-identity model. Check monotone local anchors, phrase onsets,
   quiet consonants, repeated phrases, full held tails and smooth overlaps. Adapt
   boundaries to this audio; do not copy limits from a particular past song.
5. Integrate one active lead in a separate variant, preserving the sampled original.
   Validate sample/name/path resolution with a real render. Publish coherent ready
   regions incrementally and keep old ready audio while replacements prepare.
6. Compare pitch, words, timing, tone, coverage and file integrity separately. Use
   equal-length level-matched source/reconstruction A/Bs and the same recognizer
   settings on matching source crops. Recognizer weakness explains uncertainty,
   not proof of good synthesis. Never claim an exact singer/performance match from F0.
7. Export full mix, processed synth vocal and labeled A/Bs inside the project or an
   explicit owner-selected destination. Record accepted artifacts, original preservation,
   export loudness/peaks, authorization scope and remaining quality limitations.

The original-house profile guides fresh composition; a faithful reference study
retains measured source tempo/key/register instead of silently rewriting its content.
No reconstructed score, lyrics, samples or cover renders enter the app's public demos.
