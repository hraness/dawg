dawg has synths, wavetables, pianos and electric keys, organs,
strings and sample packs. Pick an instrument, then a preset, then
shape it.

## Ask

- "make the lead brighter with a slow filter sweep"
- "find a 909 kick in a sample pack"

## Type it yourself

- `instrument sawtooth` · sine square triangle supersaw pulse
- `synth preset pad` · `synth lpf 800 lpenv 3` · `synth reset`
- `piano felt` · `epiano suitcase` · `wurli` · `keys hardness 0.3`
- `tonewheel gospel` · `combo vox` · `pipe flutes` · `rotary fast`
- `wt list` · `string sitar` · `bowed violin` · `pack list`

## Menu

- Ctrl-K › Sound › instruments › Strings › Bowed
- Ctrl-K › Sound › instruments › Wavetable · sample packs

## Keys

- Enter on a preset auditions it · Esc reverts
