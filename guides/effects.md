Effects color a track's sound: filter, distortion, delay, reverb and
more. Each track has the same chain in a fixed order; switch on the
ones you want.

## Ask

- "add a dub delay to the snare"
- "give the guitar a crunchy amp"

## Type it yourself

- `fx` lists the focused track's effects · `fx delay mix 0.3`
- `fx filter type hpf cutoff 300` · `fx chorus on` · `fx chorus reset`
- `filter 1200 0.6` · `delay 0.375 0.3` · `reverb 0.4 0.8`

Guitar sounds come from a rig of amp heads and pedals:

- `rig crunch` · `rig shoegaze` · `head lead` · `stomp fuzz`

## Menu

- Ctrl-K › Effects › Delay · Reverb · Guitar rig · Shoegaze

## Keys

- `fx filter` with no value opens its faders · Tab next parameter
