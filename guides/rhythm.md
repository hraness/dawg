Drums get one row per drum: kick, snare, hat and more. Start from a
groove, then add or move hits.

## Ask

- "give me a house beat"
- "add a swung hat, seven hits in sixteen"

## Type it yourself

- `pattern house` a groove · `kit syn909` a kit
- `hit kick at 0` · `pattern kick 0 1 2 3` · `pattern hat every 0.5`
- `euclid hat 7 16 rotate 2` · `euclid hat swing 0.15`
- `grid snare ....X.......x...` steps, X accents · `clear hat`

## Menu

- Ctrl-K › Rhythm › euclid editor · grooves · kits · grid

## Keys

In the euclid editor:

- ↑↓ picks a knob: ● drum ▲ pulses ■ rotate ◆ velocity
- ←→ turn · Tab field · Space loop
