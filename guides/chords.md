## Menu

- Ctrl-K › Chords and key › key · play · progression · idiom

## Keys

- Ctrl-P play mode, `q`: every key plays a chord in the key
- `1 2 3 4` dim min maj sus · `5 6 7 8` 6, m7, M7, 9
- `- =` voicing · `9` perform · `b` bass · `n` next chord · `0` clear
