## Ask

- "turn the hats down and pan them right"
- "sidechain the bass to the kick"

## Type it yourself

- `volume 0.7` (0..1) · `pan -0.4` (-1..1)
- `mute` · `unmute` · `solo` · `unsolo`
- `fx orbit 2` send to a bus · `fx orbit shared on` shared reverb
- `fx duck preset pump` on the kick: it ducks the other tracks

## Menu

- Ctrl-K › Mix: name, mute, solo, volume, pan, all tracks
