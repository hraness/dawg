## Ask

- "turn the hats down and pan them right"
- "sidechain the bass to the kick" · "master it for streaming"

## Type it yourself

- `volume 0.7` (0..1) · `pan -0.4` (-1..1)
- `mute` · `unmute` · `solo` · `unsolo`
- `fx orbit 2` send to a bus · `fx duck preset pump` on the kick
- `master streaming` · `master target -14` · `master measure`

## Menu

- Ctrl-K › Mix › volume · pan · all tracks · automation · master

## Keys

- `volume` or `pan` alone opens a fader to drag or step
