Ctrl-P (or `/play`) turns the computer keyboard into a piano for the
focused track. Esc (or `/play off`) leaves it.

## Keys

- `a s d f g h j k l ; '` white keys · `w e t y u o p` black keys
- `z` `x` octave down / up · `c` `v` velocity down / up
- Shift + note sustains · Tab sustain latch
- `r` record arm · `R` record replacing · Space play (counts in)
- `m` metronome click · `q` chord mode · `/` type a command

## Commands

- `/click on` · `/click off` · `/click 40%` click volume
- `/count-in 2` bars of click before recording (0, 1 or 2)
- `/grid 1/16` record quantize (1/4 1/8 1/8T 1/16 1/16T 1/32)
- Ctrl-Z undoes one recorded bar
