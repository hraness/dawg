The studio library includes original machine-voice phrases, sustained
vowels, glass and metal hits, a tape thump and a rising transition.
These sounds ship with dawg. They work offline and can be reused in
your own music under the included CC0 dedication.

## Ask

- "find a vocal phrase and chop it into four playable parts"
- "add a metal accent without changing my bass track"
- "make a sampler from my local recording and show me its controls"

## Type it yourself

- `sample library` · `sample library vocal`
- `sample preview hold-on` listens without changing the song
- `sample use hold-on` · `sample use metal-hit`
- `sample chop hold-on 4` · `sample chop hold-on 8`
- `sample ./your-recording.wav as voice`
- `sample list` lists the focused sampler's voices
- `chop` opens the full slicing, trimming, reversing and fitting toolkit

Adding a sound preserves existing music. An occupied instrument or
keyed sampler gets a separate sample track; compatible one-shot
samplers can collect more voices. Chopping creates a new sampler with
one voice per slice and notes you can play, move, transpose or reorder.
Undo removes the score edit. Imported audio stays in the project.

## Menu

- Ctrl-K › Sound › sample library
- Open a sound for preview, add and four/eight/sixteen-slice actions
- Sound › instrument exposes the focused sampler's voices and controls

## Keys

- Type to search · Enter opens · Esc returns · Ctrl-Z undoes an edit
