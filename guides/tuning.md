Tuning sets the exact pitch of each note: equal steps, ratios, cents
or a Scala file. The key still names the tonic and scale.

## Ask

- "tune the song to 19-EDO"
- "use a just intonation in D"

## Type it yourself

- `tuning 19-edo` · `tuning pelog` · `tuning edo 31` · `tuning list`
- `tuning ratios 1/1 9/8 5/4 4/3 3/2` · `tuning ref 432`
- `tuning scl <file>` · `tuning root D` · `tuning off`
- `cents <id> -14` nudges one note

## Menu

- Ctrl-K › Chords and key › tuning · Ctrl-K › Sound › track tuning

## Keys

- In play mode, `play degrees` puts scale steps on the keys
