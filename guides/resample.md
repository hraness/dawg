Turn a part into a sample, grain it, and play the grains like an
instrument.

## Ask

- "resample the lead and make a grain cloud of bars 1-2"

## Type it yourself

- `resample lead` · `resample drums bars 1-2 grain`
- `shift 7` · `shift 7 formant keep` · `fade out 0.5`
- `grain sync 1/16` · `grain quant scale` · `grain mono on`
- `automate grain-pos points 0:0.1 8:0.9` sweeps the head

## Menu

- Ctrl-K › Project › resample · Ctrl-K › Sound › granular

## Keys

- In the granular rows ←→ adjust · Enter opens a fader
