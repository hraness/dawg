Turn a part into a sample, grain it, and play the grains like an instrument.

## Ask

- "resample the lead and make a grain cloud of bars 1-2"
- "shift the vocal sample up a fifth but keep its character"

## Type it yourself

- `resample lead` · `resample drums bars 1-2 grain` · `resample master section chorus`
- `shift 7` same length · `shift 7 formant keep` · `fade out 0.5` · `fade in 0.05`
- `grain sync 1/16` · `grain quant scale` · `grain mono on` · `grain pedal on`
- `automate grain-pos points 0:0.1 8:0.9` sweeps the head

## Menu

- Ctrl-K › Project › Resample: a track, an orbit or the mix
- Ctrl-K › Sound › granular: Sync, Quant, Mono, Pedal
- Ctrl-K › Sound, then a sample voice: shift, formant, fade in, fade out
