Sixteen grooves put drums first, with bass, rhythm chords or a pad.
Each part has its own track. Mute, replace or build on it;
these loops leave the melody to you. Four groups, four pockets each:

- Machines: Warehouse, Motor City, Wire Step, Disco Dusk
- Breaks: Night Garage, Jungle Cut, Dust Pocket, Trap Door
- Pocket: Pocket One, Half Stack, Blue Shuffle, Small Hours
- Cycles: Tape Skank, Bossa Side, Clave Club, Frame Seven

`style info <id>` describes each original groove and its listening cue.
The same name, bar count and seed reproduce the same result.

## Ask

- "give me a dusty hip hop drum loop with keys"
- "start with Warehouse, then let me play the melody"

## Type it yourself

- `style` browse the starters · `style warehouse 4 3` (bars, then seed)
- `style again` next seed · `style info dust-pocket`
- `style list breaks` · `style blend warehouse disco-dusk 0.3`
- `style list all` full catalog · `style list jazz`

Existing style names and aliases still work. Their accompaniment leaves
out lead, counter-melody and arpeggio tracks where a playable backing
remains; historical solo styles stay available in the full catalog.
Loading a style replaces the song in one undo step.

## Menu

- Ctrl-K › Arrange › style: Machines, Breaks, Pocket, Cycles, All styles

## Keys

- Type to filter · Enter opens or applies · Ctrl-Z undoes
