A loop grows into a song in four moves: write notes, add tracks, name
sections, then set the form (the order the sections play in).

## Ask

- "start a lo-fi loop, then grow it into verse and chorus"

## Type it yourself

- `add C4 at 0 for 1` · `track pad` · `progression ii V I each 4`
- `section verse 1-8` · `section chorus 9-16` · `form verse chorus*2`

## Menu

- Ctrl-K › Arrange › sections · form

## Keys

- Ctrl-P play mode lets you play an idea in · Ctrl-Z undoes any step
