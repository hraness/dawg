A song grows in layers: notes make tracks, bars make sections, and
sections make the form. Name a range of bars to loop, copy or move it.

## Ask

- "loop bars 5 to 6"
- "copy the bass in 5-6 to bar 7 twice"

## Type it yourself

Loop a range while you work on it, then copy or move bars:

- `loop 5-6` · `loop chorus` · `loop next` · `loop off`
- `copy bass 5-6 to 7 x2` · `move bass 5-6 to 9` · `clear bass 5-6`
- `copy all 1-4` then `paste at 9` · `reverse bass 5-6` · `jump 5`

Name sections and put them in order:

- `section chorus 9-16` · `form verse chorus` · `form print`

## Menu

- Ctrl-K › Arrange › tracks · sections · form · style

## Keys

- Ctrl-T opens TAPE: every track across the bars
- `\` loops the section you are in
