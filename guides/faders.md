A fader drawer opens above the prompt to edit a value; the piano roll
stays visible above it. Edits are staged and heard on the loop.

## Open one

- `volume` · `pan` · `fx filter` · `fx reverb mix` (no value)
- Ctrl-K, then Enter on a number row · or click it twice

## Keys

- ←→ or `-` `+` step · Shift-←→ coarse · `[` `]` fine · PgUp PgDn big
- Home End min max · digits type a value · `0` or `d` default
- ↑↓ or Tab move between the device's params
- Enter keeps all as one undo step · Esc reverts · `?` all keys

## Mouse

- Click [−] [+] · click or drag the bar · wheel over a fader
- Click an option to choose it · [keep] [revert] in the title
- Click a list row to select, again to open · wheel scrolls lists
- Click ▶/⏸ BPM to play · the track name lists tracks · the model
- `dawg --no-mouse` or `DAWG_MOUSE=0` turns the mouse off
